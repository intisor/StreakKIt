Live Red Chronometer

Flame Tier Evolution
Streaks dynamically mutate from Bronze Ember 🔥 to Sapphire Flame 🔷, Amethyst Nova ⚡, and Solar Obsidian 👑 with 1-tap WhatsApp and Instagram status sharing.

Behavioral Contrast Nudges

Streak Amnesty (Churn Recovery)

    <TouchableOpacity onPress={() => ping()}>

    </TouchableOpacity>
  );
}
